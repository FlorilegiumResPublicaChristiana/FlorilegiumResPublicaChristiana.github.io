Florilegium res publica Christiana — a comprehensive compilation of the foundational documents, imperial laws, papal decrees, and patristic writings that built and sustained the intellectual structure of European Christendom: the cumulative legal, moral, and spiritual “bouquet” that defined the Christian international community throughout the Middle Ages.
