Andrew II, king of Hungary, for Ahalyz: the land of Widhor in perpetuity

Fejér, Codex diplomaticus Hungariae ecclesiasticus ac civilis III/1 (Buda, 1829), p. 289 · 1220 · King: Andrew II · Pope: Honorius III

Protocol Text Eschatocol · term person place · click any word for its dictionary entry

In nomine sancte Trinitatis, et indiuidue Vnitatis. Andreas,
Invocatio
Invocation of God: verbal (In nomine sancte Trinitatis…) or a chrismon. It places the act under divine sanction.
Intitulatio
The issuer's name and titles, with a legitimation formula (Dei gratia). For loca credibilia: 'Capitulum ecclesie…'.
Dei gratia, Hungarie, Dalmatie, Croatie, Rame, Seruie, Galicie, Lodomerieque rex
in perpetuum. Commendanda regie nobilitatis liberalitas, licet precibus omnibus iuste petencium subuenire teneatur, illius maxime peticionem incunctanter debet admittere, quam in idem amoris consortium, et in vnum regie potestatis participium, matrimonii copulauit sacramentum. Cum itaque nostra coniux Karissima, Regina Yoles, quamdam puellam nomine Ahalyz, ex ingenui sanguinis parentibus oriundam, de remotis francie partibus secum in suis seruiciis in Hungariam adduxisset, cuidam ex regni nostri nobilibus, Botez nomine, tradidit in vxorem;
Perpetuitas
In perpetuum / ad perpetuam rei memoriam in place of a greeting: the act is meant to last for ever.
Formula: 'in perpetuum commendanda…' opens this part in 2 documents of the archive, the first in 1220.
Narratio
The circumstances: who came, petitioned or complained; the merits (servitia, fidelitas) that move the issuer.
cuius innumerabilia tam in mari, quam in terra vxori nostre, antequam ad nos de Grecia adduceretur, cum indesinenti fidelitate exhibita seruitia, de sue munificentie bonis digne remunerare satageret, pro ipsa terram quamdam, quae Widhor dicitur, ad sexaginta aratra in Comitatu de Wolkou constitutam, a nobis humiliter postulauit.
Cuius iu stam et idoneam esse considerantes petitionem, predicte etiam Ahalyz pre oculis nostris habentes indefessam circa filium nostrum Andream sedulitatem, pro tot et tantis seruitiorum suorum meritis, eidem iam dicte Ahalyz ad preces dilecte vxoris nostre Regine, terram illam in perpetuum contulimus; ita videlicet: quod maritus eius nullam iurisdictionem in terra illa habere debeat, eandem A. cuicunque voluerit, siue suo marito, siue pro remedio anime sue plenam conferendi habeat potestatem. In possessionem igitur terre illius eam per Ochiuz, tunc temporis Sclauonie Banum, precepimus introduci; cuius terre mete sic ordinantur: Prima meta progreditur de aqua Boza, scilicet de orto Wrathusla, et habet ibidem foris metam apud ilicem. Item aliam metam habet in exitu silue, et habet communem metam cum Comite Michaële, su per arborem populi et inde tendit ad arborem piri, vbi fit congregatio puerorum in octauis pasche, et ibi incipit meta Cruciferorum, et postea meta Luder; postmoduin sub arbore tilie, iungitur terre Holmos, et ducitur vsque ad terram Georgii, que tendit ad magnam viam, vbi iungitur ad terram Iordan, et inde redit ad eandem siluam, et in medio silue iungitur terre Micha, que vadit ad Mlaka, et inde peruenit ad predium Georgii,, que tendit vsque ad Boza apud ortum Miloa, et sic metis circumdata terminatur.
Dispositio
The operative act: the grant, confirmation, sale, judgment or order itself, with the property and its boundaries.
Corroboratio
The means of authentication: the seal (sigilli nostri munimine), the chirograph, witnesses; in cuius rei testimonium.
Formula: 'vt autem hec…' opens this part in 246 documents of the archive, the first in 1165.
Vt autem hec a regie liberalitatis circumspecta deliberatione facta donacio nullis vnquam temporibus alicuiuscunque cauillacionis in posterum subrepente versucia possit retractari, ad maiorem huius facti prouidentiam, vt nostra donacio sit perhennior, et longioris temporis spacio viuacior, presentem concessimus paginam priuilegii nostri munimine in perpetuum confirmatam.

Mike's translation open it when you need it: read the Latin first
Check it against the Latin. This is Mike's unreviewed draft, and the platform's judge, reading it against the Latin alone, gave it 10/10. Yet it goes wrong where it matters. In the narratio the services are Ahalyz's, and it is the queen who asks the king for land for her (pro ipsa … a nobis humiliter postulauit); Mike makes Botez serve and ask for himself. Indiuidue Vnitatis is the undivided, not the ‘individual’, Unity. Ochiuz is the queen's court count, and anno septimo decimo is the seventeenth year, not the seventh. A translation helps; the Latin decides.

In the name of the holy Trinity, and of the individual Unity. Andrew, by the grace of God, king of Hungary, Dalmatia, Croatia, Rama, Serbia, Galicia, and Lodomeria forever. The generosity of royal nobility is commendable, for although it is bound to assist all who justly petition, it especially ought to readily accept the request of that individual whose union in love and participation in royal power was sealed by the sacrament of marriage. Since our most beloved wife, Queen Yoles, had brought with her into her service in Hungary from distant parts of France a girl named Ahalyz, born of freeborn parents, and had given her in marriage to one of our kingdom's nobles named Botez, who had rendered countless services both on sea and on land to our wife before she was brought to us from Greece, with unwavering faithfulness, and who had diligently sought to duly reward her with the good things of his generosity, he humbly requested a certain land called Widhor, consisting of sixty ploughlands, located in the county of Wolkou, to be granted to him. Considering the merit of his service and the appropriateness of the request, and having observed Ahalyz's tireless devotion to our son Andrew, we have granted the land in perpetuity to Ahalyz, at the request of our beloved wife Queen Yoles, in the following manner: the husband shall have no jurisdiction over the land, and Ahalyz shall have the full power to transfer the land to whomever she wishes, whether her husband or for the benefit of her soul. We have ordered Ahalyz to be introduced into possession of the land by Ochiuz, then Ban of Slavonia. The boundaries of the land are arranged as follows: The first boundary proceeds from the Boza water, specifically from the source of Wrathusla, and there it has a boundary marker at the ilex tree. It also has another boundary at the exit of the wood, and it shares a boundary with Count Michaël, from the poplar tree and extends to the pear tree, where the gathering of children takes place during the Octaves of Easter, and there begins the boundary of the Crucifers, and after that the boundary of Luder. Subsequently, under the tilia tree, the land of Holmos is joined, and it extends to the land of Georgii, which leads to the great road, where it joins the land of Iordan, and from there it returns to the same wood, and in the middle of the wood the land of Micha is joined, which goes to Mlaka, and from there it reaches the estate of Georgii, which extends to the Boza near the source of Miloa, and thus the land is enclosed by these boundaries. In order that this donation, made with the careful deliberation of royal generosity, may never be secretly withdrawn by any caviller in the future, for the greater prudence of this matter, so that our donation may be perpetual and more enduring through the passage of time, we have confirmed this page of our privilege with the seal of our privilege forever. Given by the hand of Cletus, royal chancellor, and Agriens provost. In the year of our Lord 1220. In the presence of the venerable John of Strigonium, the reverend Vgrin of Colocsa, archbishops; Desiderius of Chenadi, William of Vltrasilva, Robert of Wezprim, Jacob of Wachia, Stephen of Zagrabia, Bartholomew of Quinque Ecclesiae, Cosmas of Geuria, Alexander of Waradia, churches governed felicitously; Nicholas, son of Borz, Palatine and Count of Suprun, Ochiuz, royal court count, ban, and Count of Worostia, Neuka, voivode, Iula, brother of Ratholt, royal court count, Count of Keueia, present; Dionysius, master of the thauarnicorum, and Bachiens, Iula, elder of Budrugi, and Zounuki, Mika of Bichor, Benedict, son of Samud, of New Castle, Demetrius, seneschal of Wosvari, Smaragdus of Posony, Gregory, son of Bera, of Albens, Paul of Chenadi, John of Nitria, Botez of Musun, holders of the county, in the seventh year of our kingdom. “From the autograph: Spiess Collectanea (Archival Side Notes). P. I. p. 144. Schwartner Dipl. p. 339.”
