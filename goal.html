Goal and progress

FRPC is a research library and workbench for the legal history of Latin Christendom from the 12th to the 14th century (1100–1400). The aim is to bring the Middle Ages closer to us.

The three aims

  1. Read the sources well. Each document is shown in its Latin with its translations and editors' notes, placed in time and space, and linked to the same text in other editions.
  2. Train Mike, a translator of medieval legal Latin. Mike is a local language model that reasons from the period's own significations, the dictionaries and the context, and learns in a seminar against the editors' translations.
  3. Study the concepts and the chancery language of the period across realms: where and when a term or formula appears, who used it, and how it spread.

Mike so far

101 seminars have been run and have produced 193 lessons. The archive holds 12,056 Latin-English pairs for practice and 489,533 dictionary entries. Mike is at an early stage: translations made without help still need correction by a person, so a stronger training setup is the next step. See examples of Mike at work.
